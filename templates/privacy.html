Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This explains what information Kaya collects, why, and who else sees it.

What we collect

Your name, email address and phone number; a password (stored only as a one-way hash, so nobody at Kaya can read it); your delivery address if you save one or place an order; your orders and the messages you send through chat; and listings and photos you upload. Sellers also give us their National Identification Number (NIN), a photo of an identity document, and bank account details for payouts.

How we use it

To run your account, process orders and payments, let buyers and sellers contact each other, verify sellers, deal with reports and disputes, and send emails about your account and orders (for example password resets).

Who sees what

Sellers see the name, phone number and delivery address of a buyer only after that buyer's payment is confirmed, and only for that order. Buyers see a seller's name, rating and listings. Seller identity documents and NIN are only seen by Kaya administrators, for verification.

Other companies involved

Payments are handled by Flutterwave, which receives the details needed to take payment and pay sellers. Emails are sent through an email provider, and the site runs on a hosting provider. They process information on our behalf to provide those services. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and your browser

We use a sign-in cookie to keep you logged in, a cookie for your cart, and your browser's storage to remember whether you prefer light or dark mode. We don't use advertising cookies.

Keeping and correcting your information

We keep your information while your account is open and as long as we need it for orders, payments and disputes. You can update your details under My account. To ask for something to be corrected or removed, use Contact Support in the menu.

Changes

If we change this policy, the date above changes. Continuing to use Kaya means you accept the updated policy.
